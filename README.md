# HR Analytics Dashboard | Employee Attrition & Workforce Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive data analysis project developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyse employee demographics, workforce distribution, salary patterns, job satisfaction, and employee attrition.

The main objective of this project is to help HR professionals understand employee trends, identify potential attrition patterns, and make data-driven workforce management decisions.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of HR metrics through interactive visualisations, KPI cards, and filters.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The key objectives of this project are:

- Analyse overall employee strength and active workforce.
- Calculate employee attrition count and attrition rate.
- Identify departments with higher employee attrition.
- Analyse attrition across different salary slabs.
- Understand employee distribution across age groups and genders.
- Examine the relationship between job roles, job satisfaction, and attrition.
- Analyse attrition trends based on employee experience.
- Compare employee distribution across departments.

## 📈 Dashboard Features & Analysis

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes six major KPIs:

- **Total Employees:** Displays the overall employee count.
- **Active Employees:** Shows employees currently active in the organisation.
- **Attrition Count:** Represents the number of employees who left the organisation.
- **Attrition Rate:** Measures the percentage of employees who left.
- **Average Age:** Displays the average age of employees.
- **Average Experience:** Represents employees' average work experience in years.

### 2. Department-wise Attrition

A donut chart visualises employee attrition across different departments.

This helps identify which departments contribute most to overall employee attrition.

### 3. Attrition by Salary Slab

A clustered bar chart compares employees who stayed and employees who left across salary categories:

- 0–3 LPA
- 3–6 LPA
- 6–10 LPA
- 10+ LPA

This analysis helps explore how attrition varies across different salary groups.

### 4. Attrition by Job Role & Satisfaction

A matrix visual compares job roles against job satisfaction levels (1–4).

It helps HR teams understand the distribution of satisfaction scores across different job roles and examine their relationship with employee attrition.

### 5. Age Group Distribution

A stacked column chart analyses employee distribution across different age groups:

- 18–25
- 26–35
- 36–45
- 46–55
- 55+

The visual compares employees who stayed with those who left in each age group.

### 6. Gender Distribution

A pie chart displays the gender distribution of the workforce:

- Male: 850 employees
- Female: 567 employees

This provides an overview of workforce demographics.

### 7. Attrition Trends by Experience

A line chart examines attrition patterns across different levels of total work experience.

It helps identify experience ranges where employee attrition is comparatively higher.

### 8. Department-wise Employee Count

A horizontal bar chart compares the total number of employees across departments.

This helps HR teams understand workforce distribution and departmental size.

## 🔍 Key Observations

Based on the dashboard:

1. The organisation has 1,417 employees, of whom 1,186 are active and 231 have left.
2. The overall employee attrition rate is 16.3%.
3. Administration contributes the highest attrition count (84), followed by Sales (52) and Operations (43).
4. The 26–35 age group represents the largest workforce segment in the dashboard.
5. The 6–10 LPA salary slab has the highest employee count among the displayed salary categories.
6. Operations has the largest workforce, with 512 employees, followed by Administration with 406 employees.
7. The workforce gender distribution consists of approximately 60% male and 40% female employees.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could help the HR department:

- **Improve Employee Retention:** Investigate the reasons behind higher attrition in Administration, Sales, and Operations.
- **Review Compensation:** Evaluate salary competitiveness across different salary slabs.
- **Improve Job Satisfaction:** Conduct employee feedback surveys and identify areas requiring improvement.
- **Focus on Employee Engagement:** Introduce engagement and retention initiatives for departments experiencing higher attrition.
- **Career Development:** Provide learning opportunities and career growth paths to improve employee retention.
- **Monitor Workforce Trends:** Regularly track attrition across age groups, departments, and experience levels.

These recommendations are starting points for further investigation rather than proven causes of attrition.
